How it works

Clip it on. Play. Read a plain answer.

Clip it on

The sensor attaches to the outside of your helmet. No custom fitting and no mouthguard.

Play as usual

It counts the hits you take in every practice and game.

Read a plain answer

The app shows your hits this week and whether your load is high, so you can talk it through with your trainer or parents.

Who it’s for

  • Athletes about 14 to 25 who take hits several times a week
  • Especially anyone who has had a concussion and wants to keep playing
  • Starting with tackle football; hockey, lacrosse and combat sports next
  • Parents and athletic trainers who want better information

Not a medical device

Strike Rated shows how many hits you take. It does not diagnose, detect or prevent concussions, and it does not replace a doctor, an athletic trainer or your helmet. If you think you have a concussion, stop playing and get checked.

Questions

Can I buy one?

Not yet. Strike Rated is in development and we are not taking orders or payments. Join the list or talk to Scout to be considered for our Boulder pilot.

What will it cost?

Our planned launch price is $199.99 for one device, a one-time purchase. It may change before launch.

Will it tell me if I have a concussion?

No. Strike Rated is not a medical device. It counts hits. If you think you have a concussion, stop playing and see a doctor or athletic trainer.

How accurate is it?

We are testing the sensor against a lab-grade reference sensor with a university lab. We will share results when testing is done.
